## Introduction

Welcome to **AstroCurve**. We are a platform dedicated to providing personalized astrological insights and K-line transit charts. Because our service requires sensitive personal information—specifically your precise birth details—your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and fiercely protect your personal data.

## Information Collection and Use

We collect the following types of data to provide our personalized astrological services:

1. **Birth Information (Core Data)**
   - **What We Collect**: Date of birth, precise time of birth, and location of birth.
   - **Purpose**: This is strictly used to calculate your natal chart (birth chart) and generate your personal astrological K-lines and transit interpretations. This data is the foundation of our service.

2. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Name, email address, and password.
   - **Purpose**: To manage your profile, sync your charts across devices, and provide customer support.

3. **Usage and Device Details**
   - **What We Collect**: Interaction data, device type, and browser information.
   - **Purpose**: To optimize our platform's performance and ensure our charts render beautifully on your device.

4. **Payment Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Billing details required for our premium features.
   - **Purpose**: To process secure transactions. (Note: We use secure third-party payment processors like Stripe; we do not store your full credit card number).

## Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your birth data and personal information extremely seriously. 
- All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
- Your birth information is strictly siloed and used solely by our astrological algorithms to generate your private insights.
- We implement strict access controls to ensure your data is protected against unauthorized access.

## Will We Sell Your Data?

**Absolutely not.** We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, especially your birth details, to third-party marketers or data brokers. Your chart is your own.

## Information Sharing

We only share information in the following limited circumstances:
- With trusted third-party service providers (such as hosting platforms or payment processors) who assist us in operating our infrastructure, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.
- To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful legal requests.

## User Control and Deletion

You own your data. At any time, you can:
- Edit or update your birth information and account details.
- Request a complete deletion of your account and all associated birth data from our servers by contacting support or using the delete function in your account settings.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any major changes by updating the "effective date" at the top of this policy or via email.
