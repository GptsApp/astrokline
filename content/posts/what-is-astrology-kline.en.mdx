# What is an Astrology K-Line?

If you've ever wished you could see your life's ups and downs mapped out on a chart — like a stock market timeline but for your destiny — that's exactly what an **astrology K-Line** does.

The K-Line is AstroKline's signature innovation: a visual timeline that translates your **birth chart transits** and planetary cycles into a readable curve of momentum, friction, and turning points.

## How Does the K-Line Work?

Traditional birth chart readings give you a snapshot. The K-Line gives you a **movie**. Here's what powers it:

1. **Swiss Ephemeris Precision** — We calculate exact planetary positions using the same astronomical engine trusted by professional astrologers worldwide.
2. **Transit Cycle Analysis** — Slow-moving planets like Saturn, Jupiter, and Pluto create long-range patterns. The K-Line tracks when these planets form critical angles to your natal chart.
3. **AI Interpretation Layer** — Raw astronomical data becomes readable insight. Our AI translates complex aspect patterns into language anyone can understand.

## What Can You See on Your K-Line?

Your personal K-Line reveals:

- **Peak Years** — When cosmic momentum supports career breakthroughs, relationship formation, or creative expansion
- **Valley Periods** — Windows of compression that call for patience, reflection, and strategic planning
- **Turning Points** — Exact periods when slow-planet transits cross critical natal positions, often marking major life shifts
- **Cycle Patterns** — Recurring themes across 7-year, 12-year, and 29-year planetary return cycles
