# How to Read Your Natal Chart for Career Timing

Most career advice ignores timing. Astrology doesn't. Your **natal chart** contains a built-in map of when career momentum rises, when financial pressure peaks, and when opportunity windows open.

This guide shows you how to read the career-relevant parts of your birth chart — and how to use timing to your advantage.

## The Career Houses in Your Birth Chart

Three houses in your natal chart directly govern professional life:

### 10th House — Public Image & Career Peak

The **Midheaven (MC)** and 10th house show your professional reputation, authority, and career direction. Planets here indicate your natural career style:

- **Saturn in 10th** — Slow climb, eventual authority, corporate or structured careers
- **Jupiter in 10th** — Expansion through visibility, teaching, or international roles
- **Mars in 10th** — Competitive drive, entrepreneurship, leadership roles

### 6th House — Daily Work & Health

This house governs your work routine, service style, and relationship with productivity. Transits here affect job satisfaction and work habits.

### 2nd House — Money & Material Security

Your earning style, relationship with money, and financial values live here. Venus or Jupiter in the 2nd house often indicates natural financial talent.

## Key Planetary Cycles for Career Timing

### Saturn Return (~Age 29 and 58)

The most career-defining transit. Saturn returns to its natal position every ~29 years, forcing a reckoning with professional foundations:

- **First Return (27–30)** — Career reality check, often triggers job changes or serious commitment
- **Second Return (56–60)** — Legacy assessment, leadership maturation

### Jupiter Transits (12-Year Cycle)

Jupiter expands whatever it touches. When Jupiter transits your 10th house or aspects your MC:

- Job offers arrive more easily
- Promotions and recognition windows open
- Business expansion becomes more natural

### Pluto Transits — Deep Career Transformation

Pluto moves slowly but transforms completely. A Pluto transit to your MC or 10th house planets can:

- Destroy an old career identity
- Force a complete professional reinvention
- Eventually elevate you to greater power and influence
