# Birth Chart Reading: The Complete Guide

A **birth chart** (also called a natal chart) is a map of where every planet was at the exact moment you were born. It's the foundation of all serious astrology — and it's far more powerful than a Sun sign horoscope.

## What You Need for an Accurate Birth Chart

To generate a precise birth chart reading, you need three things:

1. **Date of birth** — The day determines your Sun, Moon, and planetary sign placements
2. **Exact birth time** — This sets your Ascendant (Rising Sign) and house system
3. **Birth location** — GPS coordinates matter for precise house calculations

> Without an exact birth time, your Ascendant and house placements cannot be calculated accurately. This is why AstroKline asks for your birth time and city.

### Houses — Where Energy Plays Out

The 12 houses divide your chart into life areas:

- **1st House** — Self-image and first impressions
- **2nd House** — Money, possessions, and self-worth
- **4th House** — Home, family, and roots
- **7th House** — Partnerships and marriage
- **10th House** — Career, reputation, and public image

### Aspects — How Planets Interact

When planets form geometric angles to each other, they create **aspects** — the dynamic tension or harmony in your chart:

- **Conjunction (0°)** — Fusion of energy, intensification
- **Square (90°)** — Tension and growth through friction
- **Trine (120°)** — Natural flow and ease
- **Opposition (180°)** — Awareness through polarity

## From Static Chart to Timing Map

A birth chart tells you **what** your cosmic blueprint looks like. But the real power is in **timing** — understanding *when* different parts of your chart get activated.

This is where **transits** come in. As planets continue moving after your birth, they form new aspects to your natal positions. These transits create the waves of energy that shape your life:

- **Saturn Return (~age 29, 58)** — Major life restructuring
- **Jupiter Return (~every 12 years)** — Expansion and opportunity windows
- **Pluto transits** — Deep, irreversible transformation
